# 🔥 THE FORGE

### An AI mastery campaign for a crew who'd rather *build* than get *replaced*.

> 🎮 **Built for the crew: ZEPPELIN, LEO & JONAH.**
> Three friends since childhood — an athlete, a musician, and an artist — walk into
> a game engine... and walk out having shipped a game the whole internet can play.
> This one's yours, boys. (Meet your classes in **[CREW.md](CREW.md)**.)

You and your crew are about to spend a few weeks becoming genuinely dangerous with
AI — not "I asked ChatGPT to write my essay" dangerous, but "I built an army of
agents that research, code, and ship for me" dangerous.

And you're going to prove it by **designing, building, and launching a real video
game** that anyone on the internet can play. Together.

This isn't a course you watch. It's a campaign you *run*. There are levels, XP,
boss fights, loot, and a final showdown where your studio ships a game.

> **The deal:** Most people will spend the next decade being *used by* AI —
> nudged by feeds, replaced by automation, outworked by people who learned the
> tools. A small number will learn to *drive* it. This campaign is about being in
> that second group. Let's go.

---

## 🎮 How this works

- The campaign is split into **6 Acts**, made of **16 Quests**.
- Each Quest takes **1–2 hours**. Do one per sitting. Don't binge — let it sink in.
- Every Quest teaches a real AI skill **and** pushes your game forward.
- You earn **XP**, unlock **Achievements**, and collect **Loot** (reusable
  prompts, code, and templates you keep forever).
- Some Quests have a **💀 Boss Challenge** — optional, harder, worth bonus XP and
  bragging rights.

You can do this **solo**, but it's built for a **crew of 2–6**. Building a game
together is the whole point.

## 🧬 The Leveling System

Every Quest = **100 XP**. Bosses and achievements stack on top. Track it in
[CREW.md](CREW.md).

| Total XP | Rank | What it means |
|----------|------|---------------|
| 0 | 🥚 **Noob** | You're here. Welcome. |
| 300 | 🌱 **Apprentice** | You can prompt better than 95% of adults |
| 800 | ⚙️ **Operator** | You build agents that use tools |
| 1400 | 🏗️ **Architect** | You command swarms and audit AI |
| 2000+ | 🔥 **Forgemaster** | You shipped a game. You're dangerous. |

## 📜 The 5 Rules of the Forge

1. **Type it, don't just read it.** You don't level up by watching. Run every
   command, break every example.
2. **AI is your co-pilot, not your brain.** Use it to go faster and learn deeper —
   not to skip the understanding. If you can't explain it, you don't own it.
3. **Green is free, Red costs money.** Every tool is tagged. Never put in a credit
   card without checking with whoever's funding this. (See the
   [toolbox](setup/free-ai-tools.md).)
4. **Ship ugly, then polish.** A bad game that exists beats a perfect game in your
   head. Always.
5. **Watch each other's backs.** This is co-op. Stuck crewmate = stuck crew. Pair
   up, share loot, review each other's work.
