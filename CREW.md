## 🎭 Roles

Each person **leads** one area, but **everyone learns everything**. Roles can
rotate between Acts if you want. Pick what fires you up.

| Role | What you lead | Vibe |
|------|---------------|------|
| 🎬 **Game Director** | The vision, the design doc, "is this fun?" | The ideas person |
| 🧑‍💻 **Lead Engineer** | The code, the game build, the repo | The builder |
| 🔍 **Research Lead** | Game research, market, what's been done | The detective |
| 🤖 **AI Ops / Toolsmith** | Agents, MCPs, automation, the swarm | The mad scientist |
| 🛡️ **Security & QA** | Red-teaming, audits, breaking things, playtesting | The destroyer |
| 📣 **Hype Lead** | Launch, marketing, the trailer, the audience | The loud one |

> Fewer than 6 people? Double up roles. Solo? You're all of them — a legend in
> the making.
