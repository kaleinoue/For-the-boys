## 🧬 Your Classes

Every great party is balanced. You already are — in real life. We just mapped your
actual strengths onto the studio. **Everyone learns every skill** (that's the whole
point of the Forge), but each of you *leads* where you're already strong.

### ⚔️ ZEPPELIN — "The Vanguard"
> *Real-world class: the Athlete.*

You bring the **drive, the competition, and the instinct for how things should
*feel* to move.** Athletes know in their body when something is fast, floaty,
heavy, or satisfying — that instinct *is* game design. You're the one who ships,
who keeps momentum up, and who relentlessly tries to break the game til it's solid.

- **Leads:** 🧑‍💻 Lead Engineer (build it & ship it) + 🛡️ Security & QA (break it til it's solid)
- **Your moments to shine:** Act 3 (build the game), Act 5 (red-team & break things), Act 6 (playtest)
- **Your signature on the game:** movement, game feel, difficulty, the "one more try" hook

### 🎵 LEO — "The Bard"
> *Real-world class: the Musician.*

You own **rhythm, sound, timing, and vibe** — the stuff that turns a game from
"fine" into "I can't stop playing this." Music *is* timing and feedback, which is
exactly what makes games feel alive. You're also the natural showman: when it's
time to launch, you make the trailer and the hype hit.

- **Leads:** 🔍 Research Lead (find what's fun) + 📣 Hype Lead (the trailer, the sound, the launch noise)
- **Your moments to shine:** Act 1 (research/ideas), Quest 14 (juice — sound & feel), Quest 15 (launch & trailer)
- **Your signature on the game:** music, sound effects, juice, the launch performance

### 🎨 JONAH — "The Artificer"
> *Real-world class: the Artist.*

You see what things should **look** like before anyone else can. That's rare — most
game studios would kill for it. You own the vision and the visuals: the style, the
colors, the characters, the world. A great-looking game gets played; an ugly one
gets ignored. You make sure yours gets played.

- **Leads:** 🎬 Game Director (the vision & look-and-feel) + 🖼️ Art & visual marketing (sprites, logo, the itch.io page)
- **Your moments to shine:** Act 1 (design), Quest 6 (lock the vision), Quest 7 (the art), Quest 15 (the visual launch)
- **Your signature on the game:** art style, characters, the whole *look* of it

### 🔋 JYANA — "The Engine"
> *Real-world class: the Motor — the one who keeps everyone going.*

You bring the thing most crews are missing: **relentless forward energy.** You want
to keep going, you don't let people quit, you're always up for the next rep. That's
rarer and more valuable than it sounds — most projects die from *stalling*, and
you're the antidote to that.

Here's the real talk, with your superpower hiding inside it: **raw drive is only
powerful when it's aimed.** An engine with no steering just burns rubber. So your
move all campaign is to get a *clear target* before you floor it — ask *"what's the
ONE next thing?"*, pin it down, then bulldoze it. Pointed at the right task, you're
the reason this game crosses the finish line. Learning to aim your own drive is a
skill most adults never build — start now, and you're unstoppable.

- **You are:** 🔋 The Engine — horsepower for every lane, and the crew's energy when it dips. You pair with whoever's leading a task and *drive it to done*.
- **Your level-up:** Quest 10 (Orchestration) — where you learn to turn a big fuzzy goal into clear next-steps (and the crew learns to hand you well-scoped tasks to floor).
- **Your moments to shine:** the grind — Quest 3 (build reps), Quest 12 (relentlessly trying to break the game), Quest 14 (playtest reps), and Quest 16 (you're the reason nobody quit).
- **Your signature on the game:** it got *finished*. Every crew needs the one who won't let it die. That's you.

### 🤖 The shared class: "AI Ops / Toolsmith"

> of the four of you leads it for an Act or two. Trade it around so all of you can
> command a swarm by the end. That's the skill you're really here for.

## 🎯 Your crew's secret weapon

Look at what you've got: an **athlete**, a **musician**, an **artist**, and an
**engine.**

Most beginner games look bad, sound worse, feel clunky — and never get finished.
Yours won't, because between the four of you: **Jonah makes it look incredible, Leo
makes it sound amazing, Zeppelin makes it feel great to play, and Jyana is the engine
that won't let it die — point him at the next task and it gets done.** That last one
is the secret most crews are missing. Looks + sound + feel + *follow-through* is the
whole formula. You have all four.

So when you hit **Quest 6** and pick what to build, lean into it. Don't build a
generic game — build the one only this exact crew could make, and *finish it*.

> 🤖 **Co-pilot tip:** When you brainstorm in Quest 1, tell the AI your superpowers:
> *"We're a 4-person crew — an artist, a musician, an athlete, and a relentless
> engine who keeps us going. Suggest browser game ideas that show off all of it."*
> Watch what it comes up with.
