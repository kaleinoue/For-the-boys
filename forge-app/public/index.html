🔑 Your AI Grading Key

The Forge grades your written answers with Google's Gemini AI. Paste your own free key here and your answers get real AI feedback. No key = a basic offline grader (it says so in your feedback).

  1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Click Create API key → copy it with the copy button (it starts with AIza…). It's free — no billing needed.
  3. Paste it below and hit Save & Test. 🔒 It stays in your browser on this device and is never shown to other players or saved on the server.

⌨️ Battle Keys (PC)

Tap a key box, then press the key you want. Arrow keys, Space (attack) and Shift (block/dodge) always work too. On phones you use the on-screen buttons.
